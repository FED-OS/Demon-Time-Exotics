Title: **🔥 WELCOME TO THE MESSY SHOW COMMUNITY HUB — TAP IN 👋🏿**

Hey — if you're reading this, you found the repo behind the brand. Welcome to the **Demon Time Exotics** Discussions hub: the community side of a monorepo that ships the landing site, the Tauri desktop app, the Electron desktop app, and the Expo mobile app — all four fed by one data spine so the brand speaks with one voice on every platform.

**Why this hub exists.** The shadowban is real — that's why we build our own platform. But a platform is more than code: it's the people who run on it, fork it, break it, and make it better. This is where that happens. Questions get answered, ideas get refined, forks get shown off, and the occasional vote decides what gets built next.

**How it works.** Five categories, each with a job: **General** is this thread and everything around it — repo news, tap-in talk, the movement. **Ideas** is where a shower thought becomes a feature: post it rough, we refine it together, the good ones graduate to Feature Requests. **Q&A** is for anything about running, building, or forking the repo — read [FAQ.md](https://github.com/DTEMONEY448/demon-time-exotics/blob/main/FAQ.md) and the wiki first, then ask with your environment details. **Show and tell** is your fork, your derivative, your build — show us, that's what this repo is FOR. **Polls** is where the community actually steers: which app next, which feature first.

**The three redirects that keep this place healthy.** Bugs go to [Issues](https://github.com/DTEMONEY448/demon-time-exotics/issues) with the bug-report template — a reproducible defect is a tracked thing, not a chat thread. Security goes to [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/DTEMONEY448/demon-time-exotics/blob/main/SECURITY.md) privately — never a public post, in any category. And drama coverage belongs on the [channel](https://www.youtube.com/@DTEMONEY448) — this hub is for the repo and its community, keeping it on-topic keeps it useful.

**One thing to know before you post.** The Code of Conduct applies everywhere here — keep it respectful, keep it real. Debate the code, not the commenter. The tagline is the vibe: **no scripts, no filters — but no slurs, harassment, or doxxing, ever.**

First move if you're new: drop a 👋🏿 in this thread and tell us what brought you — the channel, the apps, or the code. All three answers are the right answer.

**No scripts. No filters. Just facts, jokes, and real talk.** 💪🏿😈🔥
