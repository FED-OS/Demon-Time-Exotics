=== SUPPORT.md 5-12 ===
1. **Check existing issues first** — [Issues](../../issues) — your problem may
   already have a fix in progress.
2. **Still stuck?** Open a new issue with the
   **Bug Report** template ([.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/bug_report.md))
   or the root-level [bug_report.md](bug_report.md) copy.
3. **Include:** which app (`landing` / `tauri` / `electron` / `mobile`),
   your OS + versions, exact steps to reproduce, expected vs actual behavior,
   and screenshots/logs if you have them.
